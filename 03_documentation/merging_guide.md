# IFLS longitudinal merging guide

Before linking waves:

1. Inventory household and individual identifiers by wave.
2. Verify `pidlink` uniqueness within each intended analytic unit.
3. Investigate duplicated individuals and split-off households.
4. Document household membership and interview status codes.
5. Report matched, unmatched, and ambiguous records after each merge.

Do not assume one-to-one merges without validation.
